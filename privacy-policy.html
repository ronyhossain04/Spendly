Privacy Policy for Spendly

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 | Last Updated: October 19, 2026

1. Overview

Spendly ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application ("Spendly" or the "App").

Spendly is built as a local-first personal expense tracker and budget planner. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not host or operate backend servers to store your personal financial data.

2. Information You Provide & Local Data Storage

All financial records you input into Spendly are stored entirely on your device's local storage:

  • Transaction Data: Amounts, dates, expense categories, notes, and payment types.
  • Budgets & Goals: Budget limits, targets, and recurring intervals.
  • App Preferences: Selected currency, theme color, language, and display options.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or sync your private financial records to any remote server. Your data stays under your physical control on your device.

3. Device Permissions & Biometrics

  • Biometric Authentication (Face ID / Fingerprint): Used solely by your device's operating system to lock or unlock access to Spendly. Biometric data is processed directly in the device's secure hardware element and is never accessed or stored by Spendly.
  • Notifications: Used only on-device to schedule local reminders according to your settings.
  • Photos: Used only when you choose to select a custom profile avatar via the system photo picker.

4. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

Spendly uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements. To serve relevant ads and prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data:

  • Device Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID or Apple IDFA).
  • Diagnostic & Device Information: Device model, operating system version, carrier, network connection type, and crash logs.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, and performance metrics.

Important: Spendly does not transmit your personal transactions, budgets, or balances to Google AdMob. The ad requests are handled directly by Google's SDK according to Google's Privacy Policies.

For more information on how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please visit:

  • Google's Partner Policy (How Google uses information from sites or apps)
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies

5. How to Control or Opt Out of Personalized Ads

You can manage how ads are personalized to you through your device settings:

  • On Android: Open Settings > Google > Ads and choose "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID".
  • On iOS: Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking to manage app tracking permissions.
  • Web Opt-out: Visit Google's Ad Settings to manage your Google advertising preferences.

6. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions), and we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all transaction records are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. You can delete individual transactions, reset your data within the app's settings, or uninstall the app to permanently wipe all stored local data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
